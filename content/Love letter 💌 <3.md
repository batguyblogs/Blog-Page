# Open Source? 
If you've talked to me in the past five years, you've most likely heard me yap on and on about opensource

### **What is it?** 
Open source  is software that is made openly available for modification and redistribution. 
But what even is all that, *Open Source* is a model where the Product ( most of the times software applications )  is **Free** but also has its *source code* (the original recipe per se) 

with the open source model, collaborations blow through the roof, its astonishing to see what people can do, even without goals like making money or selling people a product.

To better understand what Open source is , lets look at what copyright and more relevant to opensource copyleft licensing 

>[!note] Copyright?
>A **Copyright** is a type of [intellectual property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intellectual_property "Intellectual property") that gives its owner the exclusive legal right to copy, distribute, adapt, display, and perform a [creative work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_work "Creative work"), usually for a limited time.
