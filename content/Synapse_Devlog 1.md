# Synapse: A Proactive AI-enabled Task Manager.

Heavily Inspired by Nothing’s Essential Space.

![[w1420.png|697]]

I wanted to build something that not only keeps a To-Do list ( we have plenty of apps for that ). But something that understood the task at hand, the tone of the request to make said task, and the time-sensitivity of any task.

And then adapt the task grid for peak output.
